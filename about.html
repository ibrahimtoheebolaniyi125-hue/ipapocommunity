WHO WE ARE

We Inform. We Entertain. We Inspire.

Ipapo Broadcast was established to bridge information gaps, champion civic development, give voice to everyday citizens, and preserve the deep ancestral heritage of the people of Ipapo, Oyo State, Nigeria.

In an era of rapid digital transition, our platform connects residents at home with our vibrant diaspora across Lagos, Ibadan, Abuja, the United Kingdom, North America, and beyond. We believe that powerful, enduring communities are forged through dependable information, meaningful dialogue, and collaborative progress.

OUR ROYAL HERITAGE

The Throne of Eleyinpo of Ipapo

The traditional stool and monarchy of Ipapo is occupied by HRM Oba Yekini Ademola Abioye II, the Eleyinpo of Ipapo. Under royal leadership, the stool achieved landmark beaded crown-wearing recognition, a historic milestone facilitated by the late Alaafin of Oyo, Oba Lamidi Olayiwola Adeyemi III.

The palace remains a bastion of peaceful coexistence, traditional dispute resolution, and community development. Ipapo Broadcast maintains an active liaison with the palace council to broadcast royal proclamations, cultural festivals, and community welfare updates.
